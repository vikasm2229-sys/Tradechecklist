TOP DOWN ANALYSIS

WHEN TO ENTER BUY AND SELL BUTTON

Timeframes Uses

  • Morning routine: open charts, find SR
  • Routine: doing same thing everyday at same time
  • (W) 30M Trend & AOI (To see if price can move more)
  • (D) 15M Trend & AOI (To see if price can move more)
  • (4h) 5M Trend & Entry AOI, For Swing D Entry AOI
  • (1h) 1M for stop loss & entries, For Swing 4h
  • (15M and 30M) 30s for entries, For Swing 1h & 30M

Trend

  • Move in the direction of 30m
  • 15m Trend & 5m Trend
  • 2 Consecutive timeframes should be in sync

AOI (Area of Interest)

Forget everything first find SR. In Trading the main thing is SR.

  • Start marking AOI from 30m chart
  • Recent SR area we are Trading at
  • W - 4h Structure is used for swing
  • D - 1hr Structure is used for Day trade
  • BO - 1m Structure is used for BO
  • Place AOI inside 15M HH & HL
  • AOI should have minimum 3 touches
  • When finding AOI see where the big drop or jump has happened
  • All Timeframe should have AOI
  • Find AOI in previous trend
  • Place line near HH in up trend
  • Place line near LL in down trend on all time frames
  • If you have AOI on the right side, it is valid only if the left side has AOI; without left side AOI is invalid
  • From where it looks good mark from that date
  • If Below stop loss timeframe and stop loss timeframe have SR then we can have AOI if not no AOI
  • Below stop loss timeframe NEW PRICE should have AOI
  • We Trade below LL OR above HH, so we need recent LL OR HH to trade
  • IMP: If you see Minor AOI in entry AOI, then mark it

Entry AOI

  • 5m should be below AOI
  • For new Entry AOI confirmation in 5m
  • Entry AOI is 5M AOI
  • 1M SR should be clearly rejected
  • Place trade near the AOI

Rejection From Previous Structure

  • FIND TRADE ONLY IN 5M or 15M or 30m TREND BREAK AREA
  • After that Trade near 1m Rejected structure

(Head and Shoulders) & (Engulf Candle)

  • See in 15M, 5M & 1M
  • Near structure shift

SOS

5M SR & 15M AOI

  • 5m or 4h should be below entry AOI
  • 5M SR will stop from deep drop or jump, avoid if it is near 5m Entry AOI

1M and 30s Trend

  • Same as Rejection from previous structure
  • Place position near 1m or 30s Trend Reversal
  • In entry timeframe: 1m head is inside left shoulder, 30s has SOS then we can enter

Last Confirmation

  • Wait for next 4 hour candle
  • See confirmation in entry time frame
  • Wait for next 1m or 30s 1st candle to show green or red candle

Tips

Tip 1

A very strong SR or AOI if it is broken Then it is invalid AOI (Applies to all timeframe)

To make it valid again see 5m or 15m HH OR LL at AOI

Tip 2

Wait for 2 more candles to form right shoulder

Left and right shoulder should be above AOI and Entry AOI

If right shoulder breaks below AOI and Entry AOI then it is invalid head and shoulders

Tip 3

Find AOI near Stop-loss timeframe HH or HL Because we want to trade near it.

Tip 4

Place Engulf candle entry in 5m

Place Engulf candle entry in running 1m trend not near 1m sos

Tip 5

Liquidity 5m and 15m (this changed everything)

Tip 6

(30m or 15m) anyone of them should be inside AOI, then (5m Entry AOI) should break strictly inside AOI to enter.

See 30m and 15m to see if the price can move more.

Tip 7

Late SOS, H&S and then after SOS it has moved a lot

Tip 8

(30m in Trend)

In 15m and 5m CTT, Stop loss 1m timeframe should be in Trend

In 30m and 15m CTT, Stop loss 5m timeframe should be in Trend

Trading Session & Rules

  • Trade in London session only 13:30 to 21:30
  • Liquidity - BUY: See Daily candle is jumping
  • Liquidity - SELL: See Daily candle is dropping
  • Day Trade: See 1 hour after the entry signal
